EVIDARA V19.1 — START HERE

1. Add your existing .env or .env.local file to this folder.
2. Run TEST_EVIDARA.bat for local preflight.
3. Open Evidara as Super Admin.
4. Go to Papers / Paper Catalogue.
5. Choose "Import Year / Paper".
6. Download the bundled LaTeX template from the importer if needed.
7. Import ONE year/paper/set at a time as .tex/.latex/.ltx, or use a ZIP when local images are included.
8. Review duplicate matches and choose reuse/keep-both for any near matches.
9. Click "Create Draft Paper". The imported questions are connected to that source paper in original order immediately.

Recommended naming:
NEET_2016_Main.tex
NEET_2017_Main.tex
NEET_2026_Set_A.zip

See EVIDARA_V19_1_LATEX_PAPER_IMPORT_GUIDE.md and EVIDARA_V19_1_RELEASE_NOTES.md for details.
